In the application, we asked about your academic or professional background,
your area of expertise, and what you hope to contribute to and gain from the event.

Applicants were asked to confirm that:

<ul class="checklist">
  <li>they are available in Aachen on 26 and 27 November 2026;</li>
  <li>they are willing to participate actively in the collaborative working sessions;</li>
  <li>submitting the form expresses interest in participating and is not a confirmation of acceptance.</li>
</ul>

Applications are reviewed by the organizing team. Selected participants receive
further information about participation, travel support, accommodation and the detailed
program separately.
